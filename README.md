# Joe Devon

I co-founded Global Accessibility Awareness Day, and I build AI tools that help developers ship accessible software.

I am Immediate Past Chair of the [GAAD Foundation](https://gaad.foundation), which Jennison Asuncion and I started on GAAD's tenth anniversary, and the founder of [A11y Audits](https://a11yaudits.com), which audits products and writes VPATs and ACRs. Most of my code now asks one question: can AI make accessibility part of how software gets made, instead of an audit at the end?

## What I'm building

- **[AIMAC](https://github.com/GAAD-Foundation/AIMAC)**, the AI Model Accessibility Checker. It has language models generate web pages, runs axe-core on the results, and scores each model on serious and critical violations. Results at [aimac.ai](https://aimac.ai). A GAAD Foundation project, MIT licensed.
- **[VisionScribe](https://github.com/joedevon/visionscribe)**, a Claude skill that writes alt text and image descriptions. It classifies an image as decorative, functional, informative or complex before writing, then gives you layered text you can paste into code or a CMS.

## GAAD

Global Accessibility Awareness Day began with [a blog post I wrote in November 2011](https://mysqltalk.wordpress.com/2011/11/27/challenge-accessibility-know-how-needs-to-go-mainstream-with-developers-now/), asking why accessibility know-how had not reached mainstream developers. Jennison read it, and the first GAAD ran in May 2012. It now happens every third Thursday of May, and [GitHub took the GAAD Pledge in 2025](https://github.blog/open-source/social-impact/our-pledge-to-help-improve-the-accessibility-of-open-source-software-at-scale/), committing to improve the accessibility of open source at scale.

## Background

Before accessibility became the focus, I shipped software for a living. I was a backend developer on AmericanIdol.com at Fox, working in PHP, MySQL and Zend. I co-founded and ran Diamond, an agency whose team built digital products for Fox and other media companies, including FX's Simpsons World and, with Telescope, voting apps for shows like The Voice. Earlier, I joined Predictive Systems as employee 40 and stayed through its 1999 IPO. I also founded CTO Mixers, a community for technology leaders.
